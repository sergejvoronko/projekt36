## Common E36 seat problems

- **Collapsed outer bolster** on the driver's seat, from years of getting in and out
- **Worn or torn covers**, especially on the bolster and the seat base edge
- **Sagging cushion** from tired foam or broken support springs underneath
- **Loose or worn recliner** mechanism, so the backrest moves or leans
- **Torn or missing plastic trim** around the seat base and hinges

---

## Removing a front seat (Bentley)

1. Move the seat **fully forward and as high as possible**; remove the **two bolts at the rear** of the rails.
2. Move the seat **fully back**, still raised.
3. **Disconnect the negative battery cable.**
4. **Deactivate the front seat belt lock tensioners** (Bentley 720 Seat Belts). Through the 1996 model year they're **mechanical spring tensioners**; 1997-on cars have **pyrotechnic** (explosive) ones.
5. Remove the plastic caps and the **front mounting nuts**.
6. Two-door cars: unhook the belt from the guide on the seatback. Four-door cars: unbolt the belt from the seat frame.
7. Tilt the seat back and unplug the electrical connectors.
8. Lift the seat out. A blanket over the door sill prevents scratches.

---

## Recliner and frame

- Strip the seat to the frame and check the **recliner mechanisms**, the **backrest hinges** and the **frame welds** for cracks, wear and broken parts.
- Worn recliner parts can be replaced with BMW parts (look them up by VIN on [RealOEM](https://www.realoem.com/), *Seats*) or with a good used seat frame. Aftermarket reinforcement kits are also sold.
- Clean surface rust off the frame and paint it before the foam goes back on.
- Check the **support springs or straps** under the cushion: broken ones make even new foam sag.

---

## Rebuilding the foam

- **Local repair:** cut out the collapsed area of the bolster, bond in shaped **high-density upholstery foam** with a suitable foam adhesive, then shape and smooth it. A thin layer of scrim foam over the top hides the joint.
- **New foam:** where the whole cushion has collapsed, replace it. Check availability by part number on RealOEM for your exact seat (standard, sport, cloth or leather), as these differ.

---

## Fitting new covers

1. **Warm the covers** so they stretch more easily.
2. **Secure the centre first:** the listing rods in the cover attach to wires in the foam or frame with **new hog rings**.
3. **Pull the edges down evenly** and fix them to the frame. Work from side to side to keep the seams straight.
4. Steam or gentle heat removes the last creases.

---

## Refitting the seat (Bentley)

- **Secure the seat wiring** with ties so it can't fray.
- **Reactivate the front seat belt locks.**
- **Start all four mounting fasteners**, then tighten them in this order: **inner front, outer front, inner rear, outer rear**.
- **Front seat to floor: 55 Nm.**
